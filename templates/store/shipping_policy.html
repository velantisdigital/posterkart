Shipping & Delivery Policy

1. Delivery Timeframes

Orders are custom-printed and packed within 24–48 hours. Standard courier transit takes 3 to 5 business days across metro cities and 5 to 7 days for regional pin codes.

2. Delivery Charges

• All orders of ₹499 or above qualify for FREE Delivery.
• Orders below ₹499 carry a nominal shipping fee of ₹50.

3. Packaging Security

Rolled posters are delivered in rigid, shock-resistant triangular cardboard tubes with waterproof sealing. Framed posters are secured in custom high-density foam corner protectors and corrugated wooden crates.
