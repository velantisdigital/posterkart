Returns & Replacements Policy

1. 7-Day Replacement Guarantee

If your poster arrives damaged, creased, or defective, we provide an immediate 100% free replacement or full refund within 7 days of delivery.

2. How to Request a Replacement

Simply email our support team at support@posterkart.com or message us on WhatsApp with your Order ID and photos of the damage. We will dispatch a new print within 24 hours.

3. Cancellations

Orders can be cancelled at any time before the package enters the "Shipped" status from your My Orders dashboard.
